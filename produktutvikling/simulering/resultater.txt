======================================================================
   varmetap-koeffisient UA: 0.99 W/K (30 mm EPP, 0.85 m2)
   holde 26C i 18C rom: 7.9 W - 15 W varmefolie holder. OK.
   retard 4C: 14 W kjølebehov, peltier COP 0.45 -> 31 W el. OK.
