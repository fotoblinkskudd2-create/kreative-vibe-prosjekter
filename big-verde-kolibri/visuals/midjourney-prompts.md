# Midjourney-prompts – Verde Kolibri visuals

NB: Ikke vis panel-/fleksemekanismen i detalj offentlig før patentsøknad er levert
(se `docs/patent-skisse.md`). Prompts under viser konsept, ikke kravdetaljer.

## 1. Hero-shot (pitch deck forside)

```
biomimicry inspection drone hovering over a rain-soaked Norwegian fjord mine site,
hummingbird-inspired agile silhouette, iridescent shark-skin textured surface,
thin-film solar panels glinting through breaking clouds, dramatic Vestland weather,
cinematic product photography, volumetric light, ultra detailed --ar 16:9 --style raw
```

## 2. Arktisk oppdrag (grant-søknad / ESG-materiell)

```
small green-tinted drone inspecting a snow-covered mine tailings dam in arctic Norway,
ice flaking off its overlapping scale-like panels, aurora borealis faint in twilight sky,
sense of quiet high-tech resilience, documentary photography style, telephoto
compression --ar 3:2
```

## 3. Biomimicry-kollasj (sosiale medier / forklaring)

```
split-panel scientific illustration: hummingbird wing in motion, shark skin riblet
microstructure macro, pangolin scales, kingfisher diving — each connected by thin
technical lines to the corresponding part of a sleek inspection drone in the center,
clean infographic style, botanical illustration aesthetic, deep green and
off-white palette --ar 4:5
```

## 4. Gonzo motivasjonsvideo-still ("Big Verde – Kolibri Rise")

```
low-angle shot of a lone founder in rain gear on a Bergen mountainside at dusk,
launching a glowing green drone from their hands into stormy sky, gritty and
triumphant, film grain, Jokke & Valentinerne album cover energy meets solarpunk,
neon green accents --ar 16:9 --style raw
```

## 5. PanicSafe-funksjonen (app store / mental health-vinkel)

```
close-up of weathered hands holding a phone showing a calm teal breathing circle app,
drone returning home automatically in soft-focus background over foggy fjord,
warm and reassuring mood despite harsh environment, editorial photography --ar 4:5
```

## Konsistens-tips

- Legg `deep green #1B5E20, teal, wet slate grey` i alle prompts for samlet Verde-palett.
- Bruk samme seed på 1, 2 og 4 for gjenkjennbar drone på tvers av materiell.
