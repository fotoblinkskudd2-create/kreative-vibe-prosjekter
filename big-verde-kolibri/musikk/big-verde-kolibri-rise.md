# 🎵 "Big Verde – Kolibri Rise" (Suno-klar)

## Style-prompt (lim inn i Suno "Style of Music")

```
Energetic Nordic eco-folkrock / synthwave hybrid, 120 BPM, uplifting yet gritty,
Jokke & Valentinerne meets modern techno. Acoustic guitar riffs over pulsing analog
synths, field recordings of wind and hummingbird wings, powerful raw male vocal with
gang vocal chorus, explosive dynamic outro. Norwegian language. 3 minutes.
```
